# 03 — Monetization Research: The Rahal Show

*Prepared 2026-10-20. Scope: what is producing the most revenue in 2025–2026 for creator-led shows and premium digital talk/comedy formats, and what that means for an Arabic, Dubai-made, weekly 30–40 min celebrity comedy show from a creator couple with roughly 1.3M followers per platform.*

**How to read this:** Every number has a source next to it. **[EST]** marks my own estimate or extrapolation, not a reported figure. Many public "benchmarks" come from influencer-marketing vendors who have an interest in quoting high rates. Treat those as directional, and test them against the Rahals' own YouTube Studio and past deal data.

---

## 1. Executive takeaways

1. **AdSense will be a minor revenue line for an Arabic show.** Plan the economics around sponsors. Gulf CPMs are moderate (UAE ~$3.87, KSA ~$2.00, Egypt ~$0.69, per AIR Media-Tech 2025 data; [AIR](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/cultural-adaptation-in-video-localization-dos-and-donts-in-the-middle-east)). Shorts earn roughly 3–14% of long-form RPM in most niches ([AIR, 274-channel study](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)). The show's 8 recurring segments are the real product. Brands can buy each one.
2. **The best-paid formats share one structure.** Hot Ones, Chicken Shop Date and Sidemen each own a repeatable format, which they monetize several ways at once: integrations, licensing, consumer products and live events. They do not rely on ad revenue. Hot Ones' studio First We Feast sold for **$82.5M** in Dec 2024 ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/12/12/hot-ones-host-first-we-feast-gets-new-owners-including-sean-evans-himself-in-82-5-million-deal/amp/)). Digiday reported that the vertical earned about 85% of its revenue outside ads ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/future-of-tv/revenue-complex-networks-hot-ones/)).
3. **Gulf brand budgets are large and growing, but they buy reach on Instagram and TikTok.** The GCC influencer market is estimated at **~$315.5M (2025)**, growing to $771.6M by 2032 ([P&S Intelligence](https://www.psmarketresearch.com/market-analysis/gcc-influencer-marketing-market-report)). The Rahals already have the reach. The show lets them sell the same reach as packaged, premium entertainment at a higher price.
4. **Structural tailwinds in 2026:**
   - YouTube on connected TV (12M+ Saudi adults watched YouTube on CTV in May 2024; [YouTube via BrandBerries](https://thebrandberries.com/viewers-in-saudi-arabia-and-uae-choose-youtube-as-their-1-platform/)).
   - AI auto-dubbing, now open to all creators, with more than 25% of watch time from other languages among pilot creators ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/youtubes-multi-language-audio-feature-for-dubbing-videos-rolls-out-to-all-creators/)).
   - YouTube's Creator Partnerships Hub and Open Call, both announced for MENA in 2026 ([Google MENA blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/connect-communicate/unlocking-new-creative-possibilities-on-youtube-shorts-with-veo-3-in-mena/)).
   - Dubai government money and programs for creators: the AED 150M Content Creators Support Fund, Creators HQ, and the Amazon Creators Foundry ([WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bhnifmy-uae's-first-creators-opens-aims-attract-10000); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/12/dubai-amazon-creators-foundry-ecommerce-sales-partnership/)).
5. **Two commerce routes are not open yet:** YouTube Shopping affiliate (MENA channels not eligible; [Google help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en), [Quasa](https://quasa.io/ar/media/youtube-shopping-la-ykbl-knoat-mena-baad-almshahd-alaarby-kd-yrbhk-rghm-thlk)) and TikTok Shop in the Gulf (Saudi reportedly targeted for Q1 2027; [EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/tiktok-shop-is-reportedly-eyeing-saudi-arabia-south-korea-and-australia-for-early-2027/)). Use Amazon.ae and Noon affiliate links and brand-direct deals for now.

## 2. YouTube-native revenue

### 2.1 AdSense: long-form vs Shorts
- **Revenue split:** Standard YouTube Partner Program (YPP) terms give creators 55% of long-form ad revenue and 45% of their allocated share of the Shorts pool. These are YouTube's published terms, not something sourced in this round of research.
- **Shorts vs long-form RPM:** In AIR Media-Tech's 274-channel study, Shorts RPM ran at 3–14% of long-form RPM outside music. It took **11,000–34,000 Shorts views to earn what 1,000 long-form views earn**. Shorts made up less than 2% of revenue for most channels.
- **Possible cannibalization:** Entertainment channels in the study averaged **$4.58 long-form RPM in months without Shorts versus $1.84 in months with Shorts**. The authors say causation is not proven ([AIR](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-shorts-rpm-vs-long-form-how-much-do-shorts-earn-in-2026)).
- **Shorts RPM ceiling:** US Shorts RPM is about $0.33 per 1,000 views, the top of AIR's country list ([AIR](https://air.io/en/monetization/what-rpm-can-you-expect-from-shorts-in-2026)).
- **Arabic audiences:**
  - CPM: UAE ~$3.87, KSA ~$2.00, Egypt ~$0.69, Morocco ~$0.78 ([AIR](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/cultural-adaptation-in-video-localization-dos-and-donts-in-the-middle-east)).
  - RPM: KSA $2–6, UAE $2–5, Egypt $0.30–1.50, per one unsourced vendor blog. The KSA range looks too high given the CPM data ([videodubbing.com](https://videodubbing.com/blog/post/morocco-mena-youtube-earnings-dubbing-roi-2026/)).
  - Over 95% of watch time on UAE-based channels comes from outside the UAE ([Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2598185/amp)). The Rahals' diaspora viewers in Europe and North America and their Levant audience matter more to RPM than UAE views do.
- **[EST] Illustrative episode AdSense:** 300K views per episode at a blended $1–3 RPM gives **~$300–900 per episode**. Mid-rolls in a 30–40 min episode help. The point stands: AdSense will not cover a 4K studio celebrity production.
- **Implications:**
  - Treat Shorts, Reels and TikToks as acquisition and sponsor inventory, not as an ad-revenue line.
  - Publish sketches as Shorts from a separate clips channel, or run a long-form-first cadence, so a flood of Shorts does not dilute the main channel's RPM. This is AIR's correlation finding, and the Rahals should A/B test it themselves.

### 2.2 YPP policy changes worth knowing
- **Shorts views (31 Mar 2025):** A Short now counts a view as soon as playback starts. The old metric is renamed "engaged views" ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/)). Sponsor reports should use engaged views or long-form views, or brands will discount the numbers.
- **"Inauthentic content" rename (15 Jul 2025):** YouTube can demonetize a whole channel for mass-produced or templated content ([YouTube help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en); [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/news/youtube-partner-program-inauthentic-content)). This rule applies to clip channels.

### 2.3 Memberships, Super Thanks, Premieres, Live
- **Memberships and Super Thanks:** Both are commonly cited at a 70/30 split, but vendor sources disagree ([MakeInfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/youtube-channel-memberships-and-super-thanks-fan-funding-separate-from-ad-revenue)). Super Thanks costs fans $2–50.
- **Who earns from this:** In Arabic comedy, fan-funding revenue is usually small next to brand money **[EST]**. It works best as a **community and data tool**:
  - Members-only bloopers and early access.
  - Members voting on guests.
  - "Ask the guest" questions submitted in Premiere chat.
- **Premieres:** Premiere every episode with live chat and Super Chat. Present it as an "appointment" Thursday-night event. This also builds a live audience a sponsor can buy.

### 2.4 YouTube on TV and podcasts
- **US:** TV is now the main YouTube screen in the US. YouTube draws more than 1B TV watch-hours per day, and podcasts get more than 400M hours per month on living-room devices ([Tubefilter on Mohan's 2025 letter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/02/11/neal-mohan-youtube-letter-2025-youtube-tv-watch-time-creator-studio-businesses/amp/); [Podcast News Daily](https://www.podcastnewsdaily.com/news/youtube-ceo-podcasts-a-2025-priority-with-focus-on-show-discovery/article_96ebdb28-e89a-11ef-add8-678397bf49f0.html)).
- **Gulf:** YouTube reported 12M+ Saudi adults, 2.5M in the UAE and 600K in Qatar watching on CTV in May 2024 ([BrandBerries](https://thebrandberries.com/viewers-in-saudi-arabia-and-uae-choose-youtube-as-their-1-platform/)).
- **Implications:**
  - A 30–40 min, 4K, studio-shot show is built for the living room. Sell it to brands as a **TV-grade family-room placement**, priced closer to TV sponsorship than to influencer posts.
  - Also publish **"The Conversation" segment as an audio and video podcast** (YouTube Podcasts, Spotify, Apple, Anghami). This adds a long-tail asset and a second set of ad slots.

### 2.5 BrandConnect, partnership ads and the Creator Partnerships Hub
- At its Nov 2025 Brandcast, YouTube said the **Creator Partnerships Hub and Open Call launch in MENA in 2026**. **Partnership ads**, where brands run paid ads from a creator's video, were already in beta in the region ([Google MENA blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/connect-communicate/unlocking-new-creative-possibilities-on-youtube-shorts-with-veo-3-in-mena/)).
- **Implications:**
  - Onboard to the Hub as soon as it is live. It is the inbound route for media agencies.
  - Price partnership-ad rights as a separate line (see §5.4).

### 2.6 Shopping and affiliate on YouTube
- MENA-based channels are **not eligible** for the YouTube Shopping affiliate program, which lists about 12–14 countries, none in MENA ([Google help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en)).
- Use description links to Amazon.ae and Noon affiliate programs, or brand codes, in the meantime (§4).

## 4. Direct-to-fan and commerce

- **Memberships and bonus content:** YouTube Memberships, plus possibly a WhatsApp Channel subscription. WhatsApp added paid channel subscriptions, promoted channels and Status ads in Jun 2025, rolling out gradually; one report says Meta will eventually take 10% ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/whatsapp-introduces-ads-creator-monetization-tools-first-time)). WhatsApp is the main messaging app in the Gulf and Levant **[EST]**. A free "Rahal Show" WhatsApp Channel is the best owned channel for episode-drop alerts. Paid tiers can come later.
- **Live tapings and shows:**
  - Dubai's Coca-Cola Arena had **23 Arabic shows confirmed for 2025** ([IQ Magazine](https://www.iqmagazine.com/publications/global-arena-guide-2025/uae/)).
  - Riyadh Comedy Festival (Sep–Oct 2025) priced tickets at SAR 100–1,200 and had many sold-out shows, though its line-up was English-language international comics ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-to-host-worlds-largest-comedy-festival-in-riyadh-1.500212326); [List Mag](https://www.listmag.com/en/see-and-do/events-entertainment/i-went-to-the-first-night-of-riyadh-comedy-festival-here-is-everything-you-need-to-know-3939)).
  - **Opportunity:** a "Rahal Show LIVE" taping as part of Riyadh Season, Dubai Shopping Festival, or Beirut summer. The ideal deal is underwritten by the event authority with a guaranteed fee plus ticket share. Recording it gives a free episode.
- **Merch drops:** Make catchphrases, the retro 70s set and game props into products (Blender Confessions mugs, "Fake Unboxing" parody products). Release in limited drops tied to episodes; Hot Ones' sell-out drops are the model.
- **Owned or licensed product:** At ~1.3M followers, a **licensing or co-brand deal** has the best risk-adjusted return, for example a regional snack, juice or sauce brand making the "Rahal Show" edition. Self-funded CPG is high-risk.
  - **Amazon Creators Foundry** (Amazon Ads x Creators HQ) supports UAE creators launching their own products on Amazon.ae. The incentive package is reported inconsistently: up to $10K Sponsored Ads cashback and up to $100K DSP incentives (Entrepreneur ME) versus a $2K Sponsored Ads match (Communicate). Requirements include 100K+ reach and a Dubai e-commerce licence ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/01/12/dubai-amazon-creators-foundry-ecommerce-sales-partnership/); [Entrepreneur ME](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/?p=41805)).
  - This is the cheapest test of an owned product.
- **Affiliate:**
  - Amazon.ae Associates: reported up to 9% with a 24-hour cookie (directory data, not verified).
  - Noon: 7-day cookie, rate unpublished ([Creator Hero](https://www.creator-hero.com/blog/best-affiliate-programs-in-abu-dhabi)).
  - Useful for the **Fake Unboxing AD** segment (real products, real links), but expect small revenue **[EST]**.
- **TikTok Shop:** No confirmed local storefront in KSA or UAE. Saudi reportedly targeted for Q1 2027 ([EcomCrew](https://www.ecomcrew.com/tiktok-shop-is-reportedly-eyeing-saudi-arabia-south-korea-and-australia-for-early-2027/)). Be ready to use it, but do not plan revenue around it.

## 5. Branded entertainment economics (the main revenue engine)

### 5.1 Market and rates
- **Market size:** GCC influencer marketing is ~$315.5M (2025, P&S estimate). Saudi Arabia is the largest market and the UAE the fastest-growing. There are ~263K monetized GCC creators, +75% in two years ([Business Wire / Qoruz](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251107367202/en)).
- **Mega-influencer rates:** More than 1M followers can command up to **AED 200,000 (~$54K) per post** in the UAE, per a local agency quoted by AGBI in Apr 2025 ([AGBI](https://www.agbi.com/media/2025/04/dubai-doubles-down-on-social-media-with-influencer-academy)).
- **Entertainment creators:** Elev8or's 2026 estimate for Dubai entertainment creators with 500K+ followers is **AED 18,350–44,040 per post** ([Elev8or](https://www.elev8or.io/find/entertainment-influencers-in-dubai)).
- **TikTok:** UAE TikTok creators earn **AED 700–70,000 per post** ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/lifestyle/community/revealed-how-uae-influencers-make-up-to-dh100000-in-just-one-week-1.500144912)).
- **[EST] Working range for the Rahals:** a single IG Reel or TikTok at ~1.3–1.4M probably sits around **AED 30K–80K**, depending on category, exclusivity and rights. Check this against their own deal history.
- **YouTube integration benchmarks (mainly US):**
  - 60–90s integration: **$20–50 CPM** (median ~$26.50 for 60s per Influencer Marketing Hub).
  - Dedicated video: **2.5–3x** an integration.
  - Shorts: $10–25 CPM ([IMH](https://influencermarketinghub.com/youtube-influencer-rates); [HubFluence](https://www.hubfluence.io/youtube-influencer-rates)).
  - Gulf brands rarely price YouTube on CPM yet. Sell **packages** instead.
- **Regulatory costs to price in:**
  - UAE media permit: AED 1,000/yr. Advertiser permit number required from 2026, with the fee reportedly waived for 3 years ([AMCA](https://amca.ae/content-creator-permit-in-the-uae-rules-for-influencers-and-digital-creators); [Platinumlist guide](https://platinumlist.net/guide/all-details-on-advertising-permit-for-uae-influencers-and-content-creators/)).
  - Saudi **Mawthooq** licence: ~SAR 15,000 for 3 years, needed for content aimed at the Saudi market ([Arab News](https://arab.news/g5dhn)).
  - Having both licences is a selling point to Saudi brands.

### 5.2 How to package the show (sponsorship tiers)
The episode structure already defines the inventory. **[EST]** The prices below are illustrative, built from the per-post benchmarks above, and are meant to anchor negotiation:

| Tier | What the brand gets | Illustrative price **[EST]** |
|---|---|---|
| **Season title / "presented by"** (1 brand, category-exclusive) | Name in show title card, set integration, 1 sketch per episode, pre-roll read, social cutdowns, CTV-friendly logo bug | Priced per season. For 10–12 episodes, roughly 8–15x the Rahals' single-Reel fee plus production. Should cover most of the season budget. |
| **Segment sponsor** (e.g. Blender Confessions × home appliance; Fake Unboxing AD × e-commerce/Noon; Mean Comments × telco) | Segment naming, product in play, 2–3 Shorts per episode | Roughly 1–2x a single Reel fee per episode, sold in 4–6 episode blocks |
| **Set and furniture partner** | Furniture, decor and appliances on the 70s set, with credit and a shoppable link | Barter plus a fee. Lowers production cost. |
| **Guest-trailer / ad parody** | Parody ad for a real brand, starring the guest | Premium. Needs guest approval and a rights fee. |
| **Performance kicker** | Bonus when views or engaged views pass agreed thresholds | Protects the brand's downside and gives the Rahals upside |

**Bundle long-form and short-form:** Every sponsor package should include the 30–40 min episode, sketch clips on IG, TikTok and Shorts, and Stories. Brands in the Gulf buy reach on Instagram and TikTok, and the long-form episode makes that reach premium and TV-safe.

### 5.3 Ramadan and seasonal tentpoles
- Ramadan is the region's biggest ad season.
- Google's 2025 Ramadan guide notes that creator content drove a large share of popular MENA video.
- Precedents include brand-commissioned weekly creator series (Cadillac Arabia's "Brightest Nights") and brand placement inside Ramadan YouTube serials (Unilever Lipton and Knorr) ([Think with Google](https://thinkwithgoogle.com/intl/en-emea/consumer-insights/consumer-trends/ramadan-2025-video-content-guide); [Creativebrief](https://www.creativebrief.com/bite/fuel-your-imagination/uber-eats-delivery-is-ready-for-iftar)).
- **Opportunity:** A separately sponsored **"Rahal Show: Ramadan Nights"** mini-season, possibly including a revival of *Fawazir Rahal*, which already fits the Ramadan fawazir tradition. This is probably the highest-value single package of the year **[EST]**.

### 5.4 Usage rights, whitelisting, Spark and partnership ads
- **Market benchmarks:**
  - Whitelisting is typically **+20–25% of the base fee per 30-day window**, or **25–150%** depending on duration and placement.
  - Exclusivity is priced separately.
  - Paid usage on the brand's own handle is a separate line from whitelisting on the creator's handle ([Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-whitelisting-agreements-for-meta-tiktok-and-pinterest/); [TopGrowth](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/how-much-to-charge-for-whitelisting/)).
  - TikTok Spark authorisation windows run 7–365 days.
- **Recommendation:** Never include rights for free. Use a rate card with:
  - 30, 60 and 90-day windows.
  - Platform scope: Meta, TikTok, YouTube partnership ads, OOH or TV.
  - Category exclusivity for 3–6 months.

## 7. AI-era tactics

- **Auto-dubbing and multi-language audio:**
  - Available to all creators since Sep 2025 (Gemini-based, voice-matched).
  - Pilot creators averaged **more than 25% of watch time from other-language viewers**. Jamie Oliver's viewership reportedly tripled ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/youtubes-multi-language-audio-feature-for-dubbing-videos-rolls-out-to-all-creators/)).
  - In MENA, auto-dubbing is already used by **51,000 Egyptian creators** (Egyptian Arabic to English) ([Google MENA](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/connect-communicate/unlocking-new-creative-possibilities-on-youtube-shorts-with-veo-3-in-mena/)).
  - **Recommendation:** add English and French tracks (Levant diaspora, Maghreb, and higher-CPM Western viewers), plus localized titles and thumbnails.
  - **Caveat:** comedy loses a lot in AI dubbing, and wordplay and dialect jokes will not translate. Test on sketch-heavy episodes first, and use human-reviewed subtitles for punchlines.
- **Clipping networks:**
  - Pay-per-view clipping marketplaces (Whop Content Rewards) have a **median ~$1 per 1,000 views** (range $0.05–3).
  - Vyro, from MrBeast and ViewStats (Oct 2025), pays $3 per 1,000 views with a $1,000 cap per clip ([Foundry review](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/what-is-clipping-1-dollar-per-1000-views-50-campaigns); [OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/how-much-do-clippers-make)).
  - Clipping is a **cost, not revenue**. Each sketch is already a clip, so run a small clipping budget on guest-heavy episodes to grow the long-form audience.
  - Watch out for YouTube's inauthentic-content rules (§2.2).
- **Data-driven thumbnails:** Use YouTube's native Thumbnail Test & Compare (A/B testing on title and thumbnail) on every episode. Localized thumbnails are being tested for dubbed markets.
- **Dialect:** Gulf and Egyptian Arabic phrasing in titles beats Modern Standard Arabic for engagement ([AIR](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/cultural-adaptation-in-video-localization-dos-and-donts-in-the-middle-east)). Write titles for the target market of each guest.

## 8. Risks and constraints

- **Guest costs and approvals:** A-list Arab celebrities may want appearance fees or approval of branded segments. Sponsors that clash with a guest's own brand deals are a real conflict. Put sponsor-category disclosure into guest contracts.
- **Saudi compliance:** Saudi-targeted brand content needs Mawthooq and content-standards care. The "Roast" and "Mean Comments" segments need guardrails on what can be said.
- **Shorts dilution:** See the RPM cannibalization correlation in §2.1.
- **Platform exclusivity offers:** Do not sell exclusivity too early. The Netflix-style non-exclusive library license is the safer first deal.

## 9. Recommended revenue stack for The Rahal Show (ranked by ROI for a ~1.3M-follower Arabic creator couple)

1. **Season title sponsor plus category-exclusive segment sponsors, sold as long-form and short-form bundles.**
   - *Why first:* It pays for production before launch, uses all 8 segments as inventory, and Gulf brands already pay the Rahals for reach.
   - *Target:* 1 title sponsor plus 3–4 segment sponsors (home appliances for Blender Confessions, e-commerce or Noon/Amazon for Fake Unboxing, telco for Mean Comments, F&B for the guest trailer).

3. **Dubai and Saudi government or tourism co-funding plus venue partners.** Use Creators HQ, the AED 150M fund, DET activations and Riyadh Season for on-location specials and live tapings, and the set and furniture partner to cut costs. Cheap money that also brings credibility.
4. **"Rahal Studio" branded-content service.** Sell the skit-making team and set to brands between tapings. Turns fixed cost into margin and is not limited by the hosts' time.
5. **Licensing and platform sales (Year 1 end to Year 2).**
   - Non-exclusive catalog or best-of license, or a Ramadan special, to **Shahid / OSN+ / Netflix MENA**.
   - Pitch a spinoff or a "season 2 on platform" deal, as Sidemen did with Netflix and Myrkott did with a Netflix exclusive.
   - Write the **format bible** from day 1 to license internationally later. Hot Ones' German version launched on YouTube.
6. **YouTube native, optimized but secondary.**
   - Long-form AdSense with mid-rolls and Premieres; CTV-friendly 4K.
   - Memberships for community and bonus content.
   - "The Conversation" segment as a podcast feed.
   - Auto-dubbing in English and French for diaspora and higher-CPM viewers.
   - Onboard to the **Creator Partnerships Hub** when it reaches MENA.
7. **Live: an annual "Rahal Show LIVE" taping or tour stop** (Coca-Cola Arena, Riyadh Season, Beirut). Ticket revenue plus sponsorship plus a free episode.
8. **Merch drops, then a licensed product.** Test with episode-tied limited drops. If they sell, do a co-branded product, or a Last Dab-style signature item through Amazon Creators Foundry. Avoid a self-funded consumer brand.
9. **Affiliate and commerce, opportunistic.** Amazon.ae and Noon links in the Fake Unboxing segment. Stay ready for TikTok Shop and YouTube Shopping when they arrive in the Gulf (not live yet).
10. **Owned channel: WhatsApp Channel (free first) plus a clipping budget.** Distribution infrastructure that drives everything above. Paid WhatsApp tiers and clipping are growth costs, not revenue lines.
